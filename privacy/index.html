Forge You Fit — Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Forge You Coaching LLC, 6082 Memorial Dr, Dublin OH 43017 · lou@forgeyoucoaching.com

This policy covers the Forge You Fit iOS and watchOS app ("the App"). Short version: the App has no account, no backend server, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your data stays on your device.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you log — workouts, food, water, weight, sauna sessions, recovery data, schedules, and settings — is stored locally on your iPhone (and Apple Watch) using Apple's on-device storage. We (Forge You Coaching LLC) never receive, see, or store it. Deleting the App deletes this data.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App reads workouts, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, activity, body measurements, and blood glucose from Apple Health to build your dashboard, and writes the food, water, weight, and workouts you log back to Apple Health. HealthKit data is used only to provide the App's features on your device. It is never sent to us, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold. You can change or revoke access anytime in the Health app → Sharing → Apps.

3. Camera

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes and, if you choose, take a food photo for macro estimation. Photos are not saved by the App.

4. Food databases

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts and/or the USDA FoodData Central API to look up nutrition values. No personal information is sent with these requests.

5. Optional AI food photo estimates

If you enter your own Google Gemini or OpenAI API key, food photos you choose to analyze are sent directly from your device to that provider using your key, under that provider's privacy policy. They do not pass through our servers. Your API key is stored on your device only. This feature is off unless you add a key.

6. Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details; the App only checks your subscription status through Apple's StoreKit.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from the App, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete on our side. You control your data on your device and in Apple Health. Questions: lou@forgeyoucoaching.com.

9. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
