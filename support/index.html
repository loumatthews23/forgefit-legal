Common questions

How do I learn the app?

Open Settings → Help → "How to use ForgeFit" to replay the guided tour.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Forge You Fit. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged. Refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

On the subscription screen tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Why don't I see my Apple Health data?

Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Forge You Fit and turn on the categories you want the app to read and write.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.
